## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an upscale, heartwarming, and deeply dependable atmosphere tailored for pet wellness, curated retail, and professional grooming services. The aesthetic balances natural, grounded warmth with functional e-commerce precision.

Key pillars of the experience:
- **Earthy Sophistication & Warmth:** Anchored by warm taupe-brown and sunlit pastel tones, evoking cozy domestic comfort and natural pet care without feeling childish or clinical.
- **Trust & Action Clarity:** High-intent actions, booking touchpoints, and transactional indicators utilize a vibrant royal blue, establishing effortless navigational contrast and instant legibility.
- **Approachable Craft:** Softly contoured surfaces, generous touch affordances, and pristine Vietnamese typography combine to make complex booking or multi-item checkout seamless and comforting.

## Colors

The color palette reflects an organic, warm balance between reliable veterinary care, boutique grooming, and joyful companion life.

### Role Allocations
- **Primary (`#835D42`):** Warm taupe-brown serves as the fundamental brand anchor, header accents, badge highlights, and secondary framing. It injects natural warmth and premium grounding.
- **Secondary (`#326DCA`):** Clear royal blue acts strictly as the conversion and action engine. Reserved for primary buttons, highlighted link states, active navigation tabs, and system focus rings to guarantee strong visual distinction against earth tones.
- **Tertiary (`#F8D38C`):** Warm pastel amber provides gentle emphasis for member highlights, rating stars, offer tags, and soft decorative surfaces.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds:**
  - `bg-canvas` (`#F8F9FB`): Ultra-clean, cool-tinted off-white background that provides breathing room and sharp contrast for card elements.
  - `badge-cream` (`#F9EED9`): Delicate warm cream used for product tags, informational cards, and category highlight containers.
  - `surface-white` (`#FFFFFF`): Standard container, card, modal, and popover base.
- **Neutral / Text (`#241E1A`):** Deep warm espresso ink, achieving optimal contrast against white and cream backdrops for long-form reading and product specs.

## Typography

The design system standardizes completely on **Be Vietnam Pro** across headline, body, and UI labeling. It guarantees uncompromised diacritic rendering, generous x-height, and optimal clarity across Vietnamese linguistic nuances.

- **Localized Price Formats:** Monetary displays (e.g., `245.000₫`) use `headline-sm` or `headline-md` with tabular numerical alignment to keep checkout rows legible and neatly stacked.
- **Clamp Controls:** Catalog product titles enforce a strict 2-line maximum clamp to preserve consistent grid card heights on multi-column layouts.
- **Form Ergonomics:** Form text fields and select inputs are locked to a minimum size of `body-md` (16px) to avoid automated scaling zooms on mobile web browsers.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 8-point base rhythm governs spacing, maintaining predictable pacing across layouts and viewport boundaries.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid system inside a max content envelope of `1240px`. Gutters sit at `1.5rem` with outer margins at `2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Navigation collapses to a top bar with drawer triggers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Product catalog displays in 2 equal columns with full-width drawer modals.
- **Touch Minimums:** All interactive targets (chips, buttons, segmented controls) enforce a minimum 44px tap target boundary on touch interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft, diffused ambient depth and tactile tone layering, skipping aggressive black drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8F9FB` canvas with `1px` crisp structural borders using `#E5E0DC`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Insets):** Default state for product cards, service blocks, and dropdown menus:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.04);`
- **Level 2 (Hover & Sticky Controls):** Card hover states, sticky bottom booking bars, and floating carts:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.10), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.06);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Appointment scheduling sheets, pet profiles, and lightboxes:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.16);` paired with a backdrop overlay of `rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.40)`.
- **Interactive Focus:** Keyboard navigation and active focus states produce an explicit exterior ring:
  `outline: 2px solid #326DCA; outline-offset: 2px;`

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes an approachable, gentle silhouette while preserving structural cleanliness for retail tables and service appointments.

- **Radius Tokens:**
  - Base (`0.5rem` / 8px): Checkboxes, text inputs, table cell indicators, and small icons.
  - Large (`1rem` / 16px): Product cards, dialog panels, category badges, and service cards.
  - Extra Large (`1.5rem` / 24px): Hero banners, promotion highlight cards, and sheet modals.
  - Full Pill (`9999px`): Action buttons, filter pills, status chips, and search bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Pill-shaped, `#326DCA` fill, pure white text, minimum height 46px. On hover: shifts to `#2557A7` with smooth ease transition. Horizontal padding `space-lg`.
- **Secondary (Brand Taupe):** `#835D42` fill with white text for secondary brand interactions (e.g., "Đặt lịch dịch vụ", "Xem hồ sơ bé"). Hover: `#6E4C34`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px border `#E5E0DC`, text `#835D42`. On hover, background transitions to `#F9EED9` with border `#835D42`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category & Offer Chips:** Full pill shape, background `#F9EED9`, text `#835D42`, bold label font `label-sm`.
- **Special Promo Badge:** Background `#F8D38C`, text `#241E1A`.
- **Availability Chips:**
  - *Còn hàng (In Stock):* Background `#EAF7EE`, text `#2E8B57`.
  - *Hết chỗ hẹn (Fully Booked):* Background `#FDECEC`, text `#D94238`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls feature a 44px min-height, 1.5px border in `#E5E0DC`, and background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus transitions border to `#326DCA` with an ambient glow.
- Checkboxes and radios display custom rounded corners (4px for checkboxes, circular for radios) using `#326DCA` when checked, with high-contrast white checkmarks.

### Cards
- **Product Card:** Base in `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 elevation, rounded-lg (`1rem`) border radius, image container fixed at 1:1 ratio. Title limited to 2 lines. Pricing accented in bold primary taupe `#835D42` with cart CTA in `#326DCA`. Subtle hover lifts element by `-3px` to Level 2 elevation.
- **Service Appointment Card:** Cream background (`#F9EED9`) with a 1.5px `#E5E0DC` border. Features pet icon or avatar badge, duration pill, and direct booking trigger.

### Pet Profile Badge & Pet Switcher
- Dedicated component allowing owners to switch between registered pets (e.g., chó, mèo). Encased in a rounded-lg container with `#FFFFFF` background, subtle `#835D42` active outline, and miniature pet avatar thumbnail with name and breed metadata.
## Decorative background

Keep shopping cards and text unobstructed. Eight paw trails mark the sections after the hero, with ten marks per trail at 27% opacity (six visible per trail on mobile). Trails enter once, with no continuous animation, and remain static under reduced motion. Static cream and peach shapes sit behind the services and testimonials content. A transparent sleeping dog-and-cat illustration rests above the footer, aligned right on desktop and centered on mobile. Styles and behavior live in `assets/background.css` and `assets/background.js`.

Fine-pointer devices use a native paw cursor with a white outline for contrast. Text inputs preserve their text caret and disabled controls preserve the unavailable cursor. The cursor uses a local SVG, requires no pointer listeners, and does not appear on touch-only devices.

## Floating navigation dock

Primary navigation is a React Floating Dock with the five original Vietnamese titles and anchor targets. It uses the supplied Aceternity neutral light/dark visual styles and Tabler icons. Desktop sits at bottom center; mobile expands at bottom right. Existing top header utility controls remain. Dock styles are built with Tailwind v4 and scoped to its root, preserving the page's incumbent Tailwind CDN styles. See FLOATING-DOCK.md for all source deviations and build/check instructions.

## Hero and booking flow refinement

Hero occupies 90% of the viewport width, centered with 5% gutters on each side, with 32px corners on desktop and 24px corners on mobile. Its copy uses locally hosted Nunito at medium body and extra-bold heading weights, with Latin and Vietnamese subsets; remaining sections retain Be Vietnam Pro. Hero spacing no longer reserves room for an overlapping form. The trust strip directly follows the hero. The original booking card now follows `#booking-steps`, before testimonials, with a 40px gap and its original `#booking-card` anchor preserved. Font license is stored in `assets/fonts/Nunito-LICENSE.txt`.
